This Privacy Policy explains how INSIGHT JNVST Coaching Centre ("we", "us", "our") handles information collected through this website.

What Information We Collect

When you fill out our Admission Enquiry form, we collect the student's name, parent's name, phone number, current class, school, address, and any message you choose to add. This information is sent directly to us via WhatsApp when you submit the form — it is not stored on any server or database by this website.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the admission form is used only to respond to your enquiry, confirm seat availability, and share batch timing and further admission details.

How We Protect Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Any details you share with us over phone, WhatsApp, or email are used solely for admission-related communication.

This website may remember your dark/light mode preference in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.
