About Us

A coaching centre built around one goal — your child's selection

INSIGHT JNVST Coaching Centre was founded to give students in and around Hailakandi focused, disciplined preparation for the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test. We work with one course, one goal, and a small number of students at a time, so every child's strengths and gaps are known personally — not guessed at.

Our Mission

To prepare every enrolled student with the conceptual clarity, speed, and confidence the JNVST exam demands.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name for JNVST preparation in Hailakandi, known for discipline and honest guidance.

Our Approach

Small batches, weekly testing, and direct communication with parents on every student's progress.

Arithmetic

JNVST (Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test) is the entrance exam for admission to Class VI at Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas — free, residential schools run by the Government of India.

Students currently studying in Class V in a government-recognised school, meeting the age and residency criteria set by Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), are eligible to apply.
